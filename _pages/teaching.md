# Teaching

My teaching at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University connects chemical concepts with laboratory practice and data interpretation. I want students to understand the question behind an experiment, make reliable measurements, and explain what their results support.

## Teaching Approach

I encourage students to prepare by identifying the purpose of each procedure, then record original observations and measurements carefully in the laboratory. Afterward, they should process their own data, examine uncertainty and possible sources of error, and distinguish an observation from an interpretation. A clear report explains both the result and the evidence behind it.

## Courses & Responsibilities

### ABCT1103 — General Laboratory Techniques and Safety

**2026/27 academic year · Chemistry laboratory component**

I coordinate the chemistry side of this course, including the organization of five experiments, demonstrator guidance, pre-lab preparation, and expectations for experimental reports and data analysis. The experiments cover acid–base titration, enthalpy of neutralization, paper chromatography, recrystallization and identification, and the kinetics of acetone iodination.
